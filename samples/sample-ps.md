## Problem Statement: Smart Campus Energy Coach — "GreenPulse Challenge"

**Sponsor: VoltEdge Systems (fictional)** — a building-energy analytics company.

University campuses waste significant energy in tutorial rooms and labs: aircon and lighting
run at full capacity regardless of occupancy, and students have no visibility or incentive to
change behaviour.

**Challenge:** Build a working prototype that helps a university campus reduce room-level
energy waste. Your solution should:

1. Ingest or simulate room-level occupancy and energy usage data (a sample CSV will be
   provided; you may generate synthetic data).
2. Surface actionable insights to at least one clearly defined user (student, facilities
   manager, or sustainability office).
3. Include at least one intelligent/automated component (prediction, recommendation,
   anomaly detection, or an AI assistant).
4. Be demonstrable live in under 3 minutes.

**Constraints:** must run without proprietary hardware; any external data must be openly
licensed; teams must state clearly which parts use AI-generated code.

**Deliverables:** working prototype, public repository, live demo at your station.

**Judging (indicative):** creativity 30% · impact & feasibility 30% · technical execution
25% · presentation 15%.
